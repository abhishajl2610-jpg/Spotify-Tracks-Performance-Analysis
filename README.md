# Spotify Tracks Performance Analysis - Tableau

## Overview
An interactive Tableau dashboard analysing 1,30,989 Spotify tracks based on
popularity, artists, duration and energy. It shows which artists and tracks
are most popular, how popularity is distributed, and whether duration or
energy is linked to popularity.

## Key Insights
- Jonas Brothers had the highest average popularity (91.00), followed by Rammstein (84.00) and Paloma Mami (82.33)
- Popularity is heavily skewed: about 29% of tracks (38,174) sit in the lowest popularity bin, and the average is only 24.24
- Songs of about 3 minutes account for the most popularity, and the average track length is 3.4 minutes
- The trend line between energy and popularity slopes only slightly upward, which suggests a weak relationship
- Classical composers have the most tracks: Johann Sebastian (3,635), Wolfgang A. (1,785) and Ludwig Van (932)
